Missile Bay

Build a missile from parts, pick the launcher that carries it, and fire salvos at a target ship on the range. Seekers can be fooled by decoys and chaff, point defence shoots missiles down, and every number on this page comes from the rules at the bottom. Nothing here is in the game yet.

Rules

Layout
Thruster at the back, one head at the front (a seeker or a dumb nose), body parts between. The launcher sets the gauge and how many parts fit.
Size
k = (gauge / 250 mm)². Mass, thrust, warhead damage and toughness all scale with k: a bigger missile hits harder and takes more to shoot down, but turns wider and reloads slower.
Flight
It pops out of the tube at the launcher's eject speed and can't turn until it clears (the silo's pop-up takes longest). Then the motor burns: accel = thrust / mass for the burn time, which fuel tanks extend. After burnout it coasts and bleeds speed (drag 0.10/s + 0.02/s per fin), and self-destructs 6 s after burnout or below 250 u/s.
Turning
Sideways pull A = (1400 + 1700 × fins) / k^0.25 u/s², plus 2600 from a vectoring thruster while it burns. Turn circle r = v² / A. As in the game, a fresh missile can swing at 7 rad/s, easing to 1.4 rad/s over 3.5 s, so a long shot can be out-turned.
Seekers
Until it locks, a missile flies to where the target was going to be at launch. A heat seeker (3,200 u, ±35°) homes on the engines; a radar seeker (5,200 u, ±50°) on the middle of the ship. A command link follows your aim (click a block on the target) out to 7,000 u, can't be fooled, and wanders more the farther it flies. A dumb nose never steers.
Decoys, chaff
The target throws them every 4 s once missiles are inside 3,500 u. Every locked heat seeker (decoys) or radar seeker (chaff) that comes inside 3,500 u while one is out rolls once against it: 40% / 35%, cut to 45% of that per signal processor. A missile that is fooled chases the decoy and is lost.
Warheads
HE 260k damage over 90 + 70√(n·k) u, falling to nothing at the rim; armour takes 70%. Frag 110k over 160 + 110√(n·k) u, 30% on armour, 130% on what's inside and on point defence mounts. Shaped charge 380k as a jet straight through, losing 8% a block. EMP shuts blocks off for 6 s over 150 + 90√(n·k) u. No warhead: it rams for 40k × speed / 1000.
Fuse
Without a proximity fuse a missile has to touch the hull. With one it bursts within 120 + 40√k u of any block, at 85% against armour, and a shaped charge fired that way is mostly wasted.
Point defence
Each PD block fires 3 shots a second at the nearest missile inside 1,500 u. A shot hits with 0.22 × (1000 / speed)^0.8 × √k (4% to 90%) for 25 damage: fast, small missiles are hard to hit. Missile toughness 50 × k^0.7, +80% per armour sheath. EMP-stunned or destroyed PD stops shooting.
Reload
(2 + 0.8 × parts) × (gauge / 250)^0.6 s per launcher, ×1.15 for a silo.
Units
Distances in game units: a block is 66 u.
