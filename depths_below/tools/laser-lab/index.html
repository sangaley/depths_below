Rules

The bank
One Laser Core per laser. Couplers extend it. A Cavity counts if it touches the core, a coupler, or a counted cavity. A Pump counts if it touches a counted cavity. Q-switches, doublers and destabilisers count only touching the core or a coupler. Everything is 1×1 and touches along an edge.
Charge
Each cavity stores 100 charge. Each pump adds 12/s and draws 6 MW from the ship while the bank isn't full.
Firing
While firing, the bank lets go of 1 − 0.75^(1 + destabilisers) of what it holds each second: 25% with none, 58% with two, 76% with four. A charged bank opens with a big burst, then settles where the pumps keep up.
Q-switches
None: one steady beam, 0.75 damage per charge, intensity 60. One to four: pulses at 1, 2, 4, 8 a second, 1.0 damage per charge, intensity 40. Each pulse carries what the bank let go since the last one.
Intensity
Lasers burn, they don't punch. Against a block with armour t the beam does min(1, intensity / t) of its damage (at least 10%). Each Frequency Doubler adds 15 intensity and halves the wavelength (1064 → 532 → 355 → 266 → 213 nm); four count.
Range
What arrives is e^(−d / L) of what left, L = 3000 × √(1 + lenses) u. On point defence the beam is held tighter: L doubles. Lenses also steady the beam: it wanders 1.2° / (1 + lenses / 2).
Hits
The beam is instant and stops at the first block on its line. A steady beam works on that block until it goes, then reaches the one behind: drag it and it cuts a line through the hull. A pulse that kills a block carries what's left into the next one, up to six deep.
Several emitters
One bank can feed up to three emitters on different turrets. They share what it lets go, so each does a share of the damage.
Point defence
An emitter on PD fires only at missiles, inside 3,500 u, nearest first. Missiles have the toughness the Missile Bay gives them: a Hornet 18, a rail missile 50, a VLS heavy 96.
