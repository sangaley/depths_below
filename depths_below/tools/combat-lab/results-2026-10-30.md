## Training (tools/combat-lab/train.py)

130 s fights over six factions. Fitness = mean(your loss - their loss). Noise,
from three runs of the same settings: -0.098, -0.083, -0.097.

No dial was proven better. jink=1.0 scored -0.052 once and -0.097 on its
confirming run, so the defaults stand. Real signals, all from turning a
mechanism down:

- recover_shield 0.05 (almost never pull back): -0.195
- focus 3.5 (a fleet call outweighs everything): -0.159
- salvo_beat 0 (launchers fire as ready, no salvo): -0.150

Most of the gain came from bugs, not dials: ships holding out of their own
range, turrets trained away from the gun-lay point, the brain and the surrender
rule not seeing turret guns, big ships surrendering at birth, enemy crews with
no way to survive a breach.
